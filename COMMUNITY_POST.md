**Title:** Gitone: your GitHub profile, rated like an F1 driver

**Body:**

I built Gitone because GitHub stats are a spreadsheet and I wanted a pit wall.

It reads your commits, PRs and reviews from the GraphQL API and turns them into PAC, RAC, AWA, EXP and a pace-weighted RTG out of 99. Your top language picks the constructor livery, and the card exports as a PNG that re-scouts itself every two hours when embedded in a README.

You can also race two profiles and export a result poster with both cards, sector by sector.

Live: https://gitone.langersword.in
Repo: https://github.com/LangerSword/gitone

Feedback on the scoring or the card design is welcome. That is more useful to me than an upvote.
